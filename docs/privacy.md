# Privacy and boundaries
Practice Room v1.8 is a skills-only prototype. The included Python tool performs local calculations and writes a requested MIDI file; it does not contact external services, record audio, or collect telemetry. ChatGPT itself processes conversations according to its applicable settings and policies. No independent account system or cloud database is provided. Learner profiles are portable summaries in conversation or user-controlled files, not durable cross-chat storage. Do not include personal identity, contact details, medical information or credentials in profiles. No public release or affiliation is implied.
